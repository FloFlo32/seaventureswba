# SeaVentures knowledge base

Source: https://www.seaventureswb.com

## About
SeaVentures is a locally owned boat rental and charter business based in
Wilmington, NC, launching out of Wrightsville Beach, Carolina Beach, and Topsail.
We offer high quality boat rentals and fully captained charters built for
comfort, safety, and fun. Designed with locals in mind, SeaVentures removes the
hassle of boat ownership while delivering unforgettable coastal experiences.
Sea + Adventure = SeaVenture.

## Destinations we visit
- Masonboro Island: North Carolina's largest undeveloped barrier island, 8
  miles of shoreline, shelling, surfing at the north point.
- Palm Tree Island: a shallow sandbar hotspot in the Intracoastal, social and
  fun, best around dropping tide.
- The "Lollipop" (Lollipop Cove): a windless, protected cove great for
  wakeboarding, waterskiing, kneeboarding, and tubing.
- Mason's Inlet: caribbean-blue, shifting-sand inlet off the ICW.
- The "Swimming Hole": a calm, hidden swimming spot on the sound side of
  Masonboro, best at high tide.
- Figure 8 Island: a private, undeveloped island north of Mason's Inlet with
  stunning waterfront homes and marsh channels.

## FAQ
Q: How do I book a rental or charter?
A: Call 910-405-2628, email Explore@SeaVenturesWB.com, or use the Book Now
button on the Bookings page.

Q: Is gas included in a boat rental?
A: No. Boats arrive with a full tank and the renter pays for the fuel used
during the trip.

Q: What's your cancellation policy?
A: Free cancellation or rescheduling up to 24 hours before the rental. Changes
inside 24 hours are non-refundable (weather permitting exceptions apply).

Q: Do I need boating experience to rent?
A: No experience is required for self-led rentals. The SeaVentures team runs
through the basics before you head out. If you'd rather relax, book a
captained charter instead.

Q: Are the boats pet-friendly?
A: Yes, our Sunset Cruise and most charters are pet-friendly.
